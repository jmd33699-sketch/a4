1. Knitwear Elasticity and Sizing Tolerances

Because socks are circular knit textiles containing elastane and natural crimped fibers (merino wool and cotton), nominal dimensions in their un-stretched state are subject to slight manufacturing variations of approximately ±4%. Once placed on the foot, our dynamic 200-needle knit expands to fit the individual foot anatomy within the recommended shoe size range.

2. Podiatric & Medical Disclaimer

While our liners are designed with ergonomic arch support bands and seamless toe closures to minimize friction and blister formation, Everyday Liner products are non-medical consumer apparel. They are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any chronic medical condition (such as peripheral neuropathy, deep vein thrombosis, or severe plantar fasciitis). Individuals requiring medical graduated compression should consult a certified podiatrist or vascular specialist.

3. Color Rendering and Fabric Photos

We photograph our socks under calibrated daylight lighting conditions. Because display monitors and smartphone screens vary in color calibration, actual heathered yarn shades (such as oatmeal heather, charcoal melange, and sage) may exhibit slight tonal variations in person.

4. Non-Affiliation Notice

References on our website to specific footwear models (such as loafers, white sneakers, or boat shoes) are purely descriptive and intended to assist customers in selecting appropriate sock cut profiles. Everyday Liner is an independent brand and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any footwear shoe brand.
