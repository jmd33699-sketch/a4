Privacy Policy & Customer Data Governance

Customer Privacy Governance Card

This policy details how EVERYDAY LINER collects, processes, and safeguards personal data in full accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR - Regulation EU 2016/679) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).

1. Information We Collect

When you purchase our sock liners, fill out our sizing consultation form, or subscribe to our newsletter, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, shipping address, billing address, email address, and telephone number.
  • Sizing & Footwear Profiles: Shoe size, preferred shoe models, and specific arch or fit requirements provided via consultation forms.
  • Transactional Records: Order history, invoice references, and payment confirmation tokens (processed securely through PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant gateways; we never store raw credit card numbers).
  • Technical Telemetry: Device operating system, browser type, and anonymized user journeys recorded via Google Analytics 4 (Measurement ID: G-0LY0HY7L01).

2. Lawful Grounds for Processing

We process your personal information strictly under established lawful bases:

  • Contractual Performance: Packing, dispatching, and tracking your sock orders and providing customer service support.
  • Legitimate Interests: Preventing payment fraud, optimizing inventory management, and refining our website navigation.
  • Legal Compliance: Maintaining commercial sales tax records, customs declarations, and accounting ledgers.
  • Express Consent: Sending product restock notifications, seasonal sales, or textile care guides when you opt in.

3. Third-Party Sharing and Security

Everyday Liner never sells, monetizes, or rents customer personal information. Data is shared exclusively with vetted operational service providers:

  • Fulfillment and postal logistics couriers (USPS, FedEx, DHL Express) for order delivery.
  • PCI-compliant payment gateways for secure checkout processing.
  • Secure cloud infrastructure providers operating under strict European and US data security frameworks.

4. Your Statutory Privacy Rights

You have the right to request access to your personal data, request correction of inaccurate records, request deletion ("right to be forgotten"), and opt out of marketing communications at any time. To exercise your rights, email our privacy concierge at contact@everydayliner.com.

5. Inquiries & Data Officer Contact

For any privacy inquiries or formal Subject Access Requests, contact our data officer directly at contact@everydayliner.com or via postal dispatch to: EVERYDAY LINER Inc., Attn: Privacy Office, 742 Broadway, New York, NY 10003, United States.
