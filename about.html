The Everyday Liner Story

Re-engineering the sock from the yarn level up to deliver flawless invisible comfort.

Our Mission

Ending the Frustration of Slipping Socks

Everyday Liner was founded in 2021 by industrial apparel designer Evelyn Drake and marathon footwear consultant David Thorne. Both were fed up with the abysmal quality of no-show socks on the market: socks that rolled under the foot within an hour, tore holes at the big toe after two washes, and left blisters across the heel.

We realized the problem was that most brands treat socks as a marketing afterthought—outsourcing production to generic factories using cheap polyester and automated overlock machines. We took a radically different path: partnering with premier circular knitting mills in Brescia, Italy, and selecting only the finest natural yarns.

Four Pillars

Our Manufacturing Standards

01. 200-Needle Density

Woven with twice the needle count of standard commercial socks, yielding an ultra-dense, snag-resistant textile surface.

02. Hand-Linked Toes

Every toe box is individually hand-linked to create a 100% seamless, flat closure with zero friction ridges.

03. Medical Silicone Hex-Grip

Hypoallergenic silicone array anchored to the Achilles tendon prevents slipping under heavy shoe friction.

04. Sustainable Natural Fibers

ZQ-certified cruelty-free Australian merino wool, organic combed cotton, and closed-loop bamboo rayon.

Quality Assurance

Tested Across Millions of Commuter Steps

Before releasing any new silhouette, we conduct rigorous wear testing: our socks endure 100,000 abrasion cycles on Martindale testing machines, undergo 50 wash-and-dry stress tests, and are field-tested by commuters, nurses, and athletes walking over 15 miles a day.

The result is a sock liner that performs with complete dependability. When you pull on a pair of Everyday Liners in the morning, you never have to think about your feet again until you take off your shoes at night.
