1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer or mobile device by websites you visit. They enable platforms to remember your shopping cart items, keep you signed in, and store user preferences.

2. Categories of Cookies We Use

We organize our cookies strictly based on functional purpose:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Required for site navigation, cart persistence, and securing our consultation forms against automated bot abuse. These do not require prior consent.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: We utilize Google Analytics 4 (Measurement ID: G-0LY0HY7L01) with IP masking enabled. These cookies collect aggregated data on popular liner cuts and bounce rates to help us improve site navigation.
  • Preference Cookies: Store your dismissed notification banners, search query history, and size filter preferences.

3. Managing Your Cookies

You can manage or disable cookies at any time through your web browser settings (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge). Please note that disabling essential cookies may impact checkout functionality.

4. Inquiries

For questions regarding our cookie practices, email our privacy concierge at contact@everydayliner.com.
